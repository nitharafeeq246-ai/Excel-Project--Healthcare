# Healthcare Data Analysis | Microsoft Excel

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing healthcare data using Microsoft Excel to understand patient demographics, medical conditions, hospital admissions, insurance providers, test results, billing information, and length of hospital stays.
The project uses a healthcare dataset containing 10,000 patient records and 15 original columns. The dataset was explored, reviewed, and analyzed using Excel data-cleaning techniques, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and an interactive dashboard.
The main purpose of this project is to transform raw healthcare data into meaningful information and present important healthcare patterns through visualizations.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze patient distribution across different medical conditions.

- Understand hospital admissions based on admission types.

- Identify patient distribution across insurance providers.

- Examine monthly admission trends.

- Analyze medical test results.

- Explore billing amounts and hospital length of stay.

- Understand patient distribution by gender.

- Analyze medication distribution among patients.

- Present key findings through charts and an Excel dashboard.

## Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

Pivot Tables and Pivot Charts were used to explore different aspects of the healthcare dataset.

#### 1. Patient Count by Medical Condition

Analyzed the number of patients belonging to different medical conditions to understand the distribution of healthcare cases.

#### 2. Patient Count by Admission Type

Examined patient admissions based on three categories:

Emergency

Elective

Urgent

This analysis helps understand the distribution of hospital admission types.

#### 3. Patient Count by Insurance Provider

Analyzed patient distribution across different insurance providers to understand insurance coverage patterns.

#### 4. Medical Condition by Admission Type

Compared medical conditions across different admission types to identify patterns in patient admissions.

#### 5. Monthly Hospital Admissions

Analyzed admission dates by month to understand monthly patient admission trends.

#### 6. Patient Count by Test Results

Examined the distribution of test results across three categories:

Normal

Abnormal

Inconclusive

#### 7. Billing Amount and Length of Stay

Explored the relationship between hospital billing amounts and the duration of patient hospital stays.

This analysis helps examine how billing patterns vary across different lengths of stay.

#### 8. Patient Distribution by Gender

Analyzed the distribution of patients based on gender to understand the demographic composition of the dataset.

#### 9. Patient Count by Medication

Examined patient distribution across different medication categories to understand medication usage patterns.
